# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, inclusive, and constructive place for collaboration, learning, and research.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Welcome good-faith questions and contributions from people with different backgrounds.
- Give credit to upstream authors, contributors, and third-party work.
- Report security issues and personal data responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled privately and in good faith. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
